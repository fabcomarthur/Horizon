## [Google Ads Clicks Lose Paid Attribution Due to Stripped Tracking Parameters](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMilgFBVV95cUxPOXBTRnZaTGFyUTNzTEQySF9KV1ZndTl3d200SVlVQU15YTFsNXNCMWJuMVdZTi1vVE9ZbWRLZ0pEbkVrLVJ1OTlnb3JETVM3WDB4SUVTZlBGVF84eEVvNzlBNVhIOTFYcUpIS3dMRkxMY0hTTW9UWk93UjQ3VVk2ZDVMUXBNd1psN2tONHFPR0duQlo0Snc?oc=5) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A technical issue is causing Google Ads clicks to lose their paid attribution when essential tracking parameters, specifically GBRAID and gad_, are stripped from the URLs, directly impacting the accuracy of conversion measurement. This issue is significant for advertisers as it leads to inaccurate reporting of Google Ads campaign performance and can result in misinformed optimization decisions, potentially wasting ad spend and hindering effective campaign management. The stripping of GBRAID and gad_ parameters prevents proper tracking of conversions, especially in privacy-restricted environments where GBRAID is used for iOS app and web conversions, while gad_ parameters provide aggregate campaign data.

rss · Google News — Ad Platforms · Aug 2, 08:44

**Background**: GBRAID is a privacy-preserving URL parameter introduced by Google to measure ad performance and conversions, particularly in environments with restricted traditional tracking mechanisms like iOS 14+; it helps attribute app and web conversions without linking to individual users. The gad_ parameters are also URL parameters that provide aggregate data about Google Ads campaigns, offering valuable insights into performance while maintaining user privacy.
