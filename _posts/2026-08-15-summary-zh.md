## [谷歌将 Gemini 3.7 Flash 集成到搜索 AI 模式，提升 AI 概览速度](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimwFBVV95cUxQSGlWQjNyLUZwOHhvd1YwYjA0b3lXWkFNUUY4cmR6blFBdUtndWltMWJUbDFoblBQQS1qLVlvaHdvdWZ2bmpIQU1KSlNpS293RFRtQ3dkQ3Frb2pXZlhRRTFENlBMMldQc203WGotSjdOYVJsZVJDemMycWNUdXJGcmhQNUh2NXMwSXF5QWZEa1NSVFU3SWRZYS10SQ?oc=5) ⭐️ 7.0/10
