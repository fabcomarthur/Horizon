The Trade Desk has partnered with Gracenote to integrate show-level Connected TV (CTV) data, marking Gracenote's inaugural deal with a Demand-Side Platform (DSP). This collaboration provides advertisers with more granular data for precise targeting and measurement within CTV campaigns. This partnership significantly enhances advertisers' ability to target and measure campaigns on Connected TV (CTV) through The Trade Desk, leading to improved campaign performance and more effective ad spending. It marks a crucial step in advancing programmatic advertising capabilities within the rapidly growing CTV ecosystem. The core technical detail is the integration of "show-level" CTV data, allowing advertisers to target specific programs and content rather than just broader channels or genres. This granular data enables more precise audience segmentation, improved ad relevance, and better attribution modeling for CTV campaigns.

**Background**: Gracenote is a company known for providing extensive music, video, and sports metadata and content recognition technologies to entertainment services globally. A Demand-Side Platform (DSP) is a programmatic advertising technology that enables advertisers to manage and automate the buying of digital ad impressions across various ad exchanges. Connected TV (CTV) refers to televisions that are connected to the internet, allowing users to stream video content from various apps and services.
