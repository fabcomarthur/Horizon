South Korean regulators will require brokerages and asset managers to submit their YouTube advertisements for pre-review starting January. This new mandate aims to overhaul financial investment ad rules, specifically targeting ads for investment products like ETFs and other high-risk offerings. This regulatory change is significant as it directly impacts how financial services firms in South Korea plan, buy, and execute advertising campaigns on major platforms like YouTube. It could lead to increased compliance costs and longer lead times for ad approvals, potentially affecting marketing strategies in a key APAC market. The Financial Supervisory Service (FSS) is spearheading this overhaul of financial investment ad rules, with a particular focus on advertisements for high-risk products and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). This move extends regulatory oversight to digital platforms, ensuring consumer protection in online financial promotions.
